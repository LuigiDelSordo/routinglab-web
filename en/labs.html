Packet Tracer labs for the CCNA

Fifteen labs to build in Cisco Packet Tracer, with the topology, addressing, tasks and an explained solution. They are the same labs as in the app, where you can also do them on your phone.

How to get the most out of them

extension

Build the topology

Place the devices, cable them as the table says and apply the starting configuration.

terminal

Solve it blind

Try each task on your own. The solution stays hidden until you ask for it.

check_circle

Verify

Compare your show and ping output with the expected output in each guide.
